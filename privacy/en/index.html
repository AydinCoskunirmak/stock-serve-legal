Who we are

Stock & Serve ("the Game") is a mobile game for iOS and Android developed by Aydın Coşkunırmak, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game and what choices you have.

Summary

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number. Most of your game progress is stored on your device. To run the online leaderboard and to understand how the Game is played, we use Google Firebase, which receives a random anonymous player ID, the player name and avatar you choose, your score and basic progress, and anonymous usage events. The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google may process device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure those ads, depending on the choices you make.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, levels, stars, purchased restaurants, settings such as sound, music and language, and the player name and avatar you choose) is saved locally on your device. If you delete the Game, this local data is deleted too. You can also erase it at any time in Settings > Reset progress.

Online leaderboard and analytics (Google Firebase)

When you play, the Game signs in to Google Firebase with a random anonymous ID (Firebase Anonymous Authentication). This ID is not linked to your name, email address or Apple/Google account. For the all-time leaderboard we store under this ID, in Google Cloud Firestore, the player name and avatar you choose, your total satisfaction score, the number of levels passed, your highest level, the number of restaurants opened, the last restaurant played and whether you completed the tutorial. Your player name, avatar and score are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your player name.

We also use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is used and to improve it. It records events such as starting and finishing a level, opening a restaurant, completing a purchase and watching a rewarded ad, together with basic device information (such as device model, operating system, app version, language and approximate country derived from your IP address). Analytics data is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared with data brokers. Google processes this data on our behalf; see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads (which you choose to watch for an in-game reward) and occasional interstitial ads. To serve, limit, measure and prevent fraud in these ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device model and operating system, app usage and ad interactions, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

On iOS, the advertising identifier is only used for tracking if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Google's processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases (for example coin packs or removing interstitial ads) are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the item.

Ratings and sharing

If you choose to rate the Game or share it, this happens through the standard Apple, Google or system share screens. We do not receive any information about you from these actions.

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as the online leaderboard is operated, or until you ask us to delete them. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase settings (at most 14 months) and then deleted automatically. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data and to withdraw consent. You can withdraw ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy options, limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Google > Ads), or contact us with any request. To delete your leaderboard entry, email us your player name and, if possible, the approximate date you last played, and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update shown above.
